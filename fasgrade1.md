What they're validating: Pipeline vs. revenue. Iterative will push hard here.

Q18: "150+ active talents benchmarked — are these paying users? What's the retention or repeat usage?"

What they're validating: Whether 150 is a real user base or a one-time pilot cohort.

Q19: "What's your growth rate month-over-month right now?"

What they're validating: Momentum. If you can't answer this crisply, it's a red flag.

Q20: "You had a POC with 100+ students. What happened after that? Did the university convert to a paid contract? If not, why?"

What they're validating: POC-to-conversion rate, which is the #1 killer of enterprise startups.

6. PIVOT & STRATEGY
Q21: "You moved away from a job platform and EOR service. Why? What failed? What did you learn from that experience?"

What they're validating: Self-awareness, learning velocity, whether this pivot is conviction-driven or desperation-driven.

Q22: "Your last application was presumably about Kukerja or a different model. Why should we believe this version will work when the previous one didn't?"

What they're validating: Pattern recognition — are you iterating toward product-market fit or just hopping between ideas?

7. AI & TECHNOLOGY
Q23: "You're in the Claude for Startups program. That gives you credits. But what happens when credits run out? What are your AI infrastructure costs per assessment? Is the unit economics viable at scale?"

What they're validating: Whether your business model survives without subsidized AI compute.

Q24: "If Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google builds a general-purpose skill assessment tool tomorrow, what's your moat? Isn't this an 'AI wrapper' risk?"

What they're validating: Defensibility beyond the AI layer.

Q25: "What proprietary data or models do you have that improve over time and create a compounding advantage?"

What they're validating: Network effects, data moats, or flywheel dynamics.

8. FUNDRAISING & USE OF FUNDS
Q26: "How much are you raising, and what will you use it for? What milestones will you hit with this capital?"

What they're validating: Capital efficiency and clarity of next milestones.

Q27: "What's your burn rate? How long is your runway?"

What they're validating: Urgency and financial discipline.

9. HARD / UNCOMFORTABLE QUESTIONS
Q28: "Be honest — do you have any paying customers right now? If yes, how much are they paying? If no, when do you expect first revenue?"

Q29: "Your application mentions a lot of 'potential contracts' and 'ongoing discussions.' In our experience, pipeline means nothing until signed. What's actually closed?"

Q30: "If I gave you $150K today, what's the ONE thing you'd do with it that would prove or disprove your thesis in 90 days?"

MY RECOMMENDATIONS FOR YOUR PREPARATION:
Get your revenue number crisp. Even if it's $0, say "$0 but here's why we'll be at $X by month Y." Vagueness kills applications.

Narrow your focus. Pick ONE of your revenue streams as the primary wedge. I'd suggest: university pre-certification (scalable, repeatable, lower sales cycle than enterprise).

Prepare the "after POC" story. They will hammer you on POC-to-paid conversion. If Health Polytechnic hasn't signed a paid contract, explain exactly what's blocking it and what you're doing about it.

Quantify the Kukerja advantage. "Established database" — how many contacts? How many enterprises? How many have you actually converted through this channel?

Have your unit economics per assessment ready. Cost to deliver one assessment (AI compute + platform + support) vs. revenue per assessment. Show it's a positive-margin business.

Prepare a clear answer on AI moat. The "Claude for Startups" backing is not a moat — it's a cost subsidy. Your moat needs to be proprietary assessment frameworks, data, or distribution.

🔴 ROUND 1: The Pivot & Founder-Market Fit
Q1: "You evolved from Kukerja — a job platform and EOR service. What exactly failed in that model, and what was the specific moment you knew you had to pivot?"

Why they ask: Mereka ingin melihat apakah kamu jujur tentang kegagalan/stagnasi sebelumnya dan apakah pivot ini data-driven atau hanya mengejar tren AI.

Q2: "What is the legal and operational relationship between Kukerja and Fasgrade? Are they the same entity? How is the cap table structured?"

Why they ask: VC sering mundur kalau ada masalah struktur perusahaan. Mereka perlu tahu apakah investor Fasgrade harus deal dengan legacy Kukerja investors.

Q3: "You were Best National Sales at Samsung. That's a structured corporate environment. How does that translate to early-stage founder hustle and building an AI software product?"

Why they ask: Founder-market fit — apakah skill sales corporate bisa ditranslasikan ke product building dan startup execution?

Q4: "Who else is on your founding team? Do you have a technical co-founder? Who actually built the product?"

Why they ask: Solo founder risk dan technical capability. Tanpa CTO, bagaimana kamu bisa membangun AI-native product yang defensible?

Q5: "You're running both Kukerja and Fasgrade. How do you split your time, and is there a conflict of interest?"

Why they ask: Focus. Investor mau tahu kamu 100% committed ke Fasgrade.

Q8: "How does the AI actually evaluate a salesperson's ability to handle rejection or objections under pressure? Give me one specific example of an assessment scenario and how the AI grades it."

Why they ask: Memvalidasi apakah ini benar-benar AI-native assessment atau hanya quiz pilihan ganda yang digenerate LLM.

Why they ask: Ini pertanyaan khas Iterative — mereka ingin melihat focused execution thinking dan capital efficiency.

💡 Tips Strategis untuk Interview
1. Jangan Defensive — Jawab dengan Data
Kalau partner bilang "I think selling to universities is terrible," jangan ngegas. Jawab: "We thought the same at first, BUT the data from our PGRI POC showed us that..."

2. Kuasai Counter-Intuitive Insight
Kalimat emas di application kamu: "Enterprises don't want prolonged training... they want adaptive decision-making assets." Ulangi thesis ini — ini menunjukkan kamu deep dive ke customer.

3. Siapkan Angka Spesifik
Ganti "many," "fast," "strong pipeline" dengan "150 talents," "7 days vs. 49 days," "$X pipeline with Y% probability."

4. Pilih SATU Revenue Stream Utama
Kalau ditanya 4 stream, bilang yang mana prioritas sekarang dan kenapa. Recommended: university pre-certification (scalable, repeatable, shorter sales cycle than enterprise).

5. Address the Kukerja Relationship Proactively
"Kukerja gave us the market insight and database. Fasgrade is the high-margin, software-driven layer that Kukerja was missing. They are [same entity / separate entity], and here's why that's clean for investors..."

6. AI Moat ≠ API Access
Jawaban kuat: "Our moat isn't Claude access — it's our proprietary assessment framework built on [X] sales competency dimensions, validated against [Y] real job outcomes, plus our growing dataset of [Z] candidate interactions that trains our scoring model to be more accurate over time."
